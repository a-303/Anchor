          How this app works
          • Today shows the one next thing in green. Tap Done or Minimum and the next one appears.
          • Full, Min, ✕ mean the full version, the minimum version, or missed. Tap the same button again to undo. Tap a habit's name to see what full and minimum are.
          • Your three come from last night's journal. Start opens a timer on that item.
          • Stuck? is the 3-minute unstick and the timer. Every timer counts as focus time.
          • Got free time? hands you one thing for the time you have.
          • People and For fun are once a week, with no minimum. They are never late and never missed. They are there so the plan is not only duties.
          • Journal: one line at night is a full pass. The arrows let you fill in an earlier day.
          • Body: one short routine for the time you have, and the swim log.
          • Progress: the week grid and Sunday review, the trends, and the log of everything you did.
          • Minimum day and travel mode: the minimum becomes the plan, and it still counts.
          • The day ends at 03:00, so a late night still logs on the right day.
